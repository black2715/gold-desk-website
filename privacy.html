Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gold Desk (“the application”) handles information when you use its desktop application and optional Google Drive integration.

1. Personal use

Gold Desk is a privately maintained desktop application intended for personal record-keeping. It is not operated as a public financial service, and this website does not provide user accounts or collect information through forms.

2. Information you choose to manage

Information you enter or store in Gold Desk may include financial records, notes, and supporting photos or files. Such information is intended for your use within the application. The exact storage location depends on the application's configured features and settings.

3. Google Drive integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Gold Desk requests Google authorization to use the Drive functionality it provides. Depending on the enabled features and permissions, the application may access or manage files that you select or that it creates for its Drive integration.

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are used to maintain your authorized connection. The desktop application is designed to protect saved credentials using operating-system secure storage. You can disconnect Google Drive from the application's settings or revoke access through your Google Account security settings.

Gold Desk does not sell Google Drive data or use it for advertising. Google services are subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

4. Data storage and security

Gold Desk is designed to keep your information under your control. Information may be stored locally on your device and, when you enable cloud features, in your Google Drive account. Security measures depend on the application version, operating system, and services you configure. Keep your device and Google Account secure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Gold Desk does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be transmitted to services you explicitly connect (such as Google Drive) to provide the functionality you request. This website does not intentionally collect or share visitor personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

You control records stored in the application and files stored in your connected Google Drive account. Removing the application or disconnecting Google Drive may not automatically delete files previously stored in Drive. Review and delete those files separately if you wish.

7. Permissions and revoking access

You may disconnect Google Drive from Gold Desk settings. You may also review or revoke the application's access in your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents the application from using Google Drive until you authorize it again.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions about Gold Desk, contact the developer using the support email address listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.

This policy is a general description of the intended privacy practices. It should be reviewed and updated if the application's actual data flows, permissions, or storage services change.
